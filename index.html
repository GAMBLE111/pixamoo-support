Support

Pixamoo makes and edits pictures with AI that runs on your iPhone. Need help? Email SUPPORT_EMAIL and we'll get back to you. Including your iPhone model helps (Settings › Contact support in the app adds it for you).

Common questions

Which iPhones does Pixamoo work on?

iPhone 15 Pro and newer, on iOS 26.4 or later. The AI needs the memory these phones have.

Why is the download so big?

The AI models (about 4 GB) come with the app, so everything runs on your phone, works offline and nothing you make is uploaded. If the models didn't finish downloading, the app offers to finish it, Wi-Fi recommended.

How long does a picture take?

About half a minute for a new picture on an iPhone 16 Pro, longer for photo edits. The first one after opening the app takes longer while the AI gets ready. Keep Pixamoo open while it works.

Why was my prompt blocked?

Every prompt is checked against our content rules (Settings › Community guidelines). If you think it was blocked by mistake, tap "I think this is a mistake" on that screen.

How do I report a picture?

Open the picture's Share menu and choose Report this image, or email us.

How do I delete my account?

Settings › Account › Delete account. Your pictures stay on your phone.
