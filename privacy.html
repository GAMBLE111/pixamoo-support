Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Pixamoo makes and edits pictures with an AI model that runs on your iPhone. Your pictures and photos never leave your phone unless you share them yourself. This policy explains the small amount of information that does go to our server, and why.

Who we are

Pixamoo is made by SUPPORT_NAME. You can reach us at SUPPORT_EMAIL.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Every picture you make, and every photo you edit. The AI runs on the phone; images are not uploaded.
  • Your Library, favourites and settings.
  • Photos you pick from your camera roll or take with the camera. Pixamoo only reads the ones you choose.

What we collect

InformationWhy
Your email addressTo sign you in with a one-time code.
An account ID and the handle you pickTo keep your account and show your name in the app.
Whether you're in Australia and under 16 (yes or no)To keep features meant for older users switched off. Your age range itself stays on the phone.

We do not collect your pictures, your photos, your location, your contacts or anything from other apps. We do not use advertising, analytics or tracking, and we never sell or share your information with advertisers or data brokers.

Where it's kept

Account information and prompts are stored with our hosting provider, Supabase, which processes it for us under its own data protection terms. It may be stored outside your country.

How long we keep it

We keep your account information and prompts while you have an account. When you delete your account, they are deleted. Prompts linked to a safety report may be kept for longer where the law requires it.

Your choices

  • Delete your account at any time in Settings › Account › Delete account. Your pictures stay on your phone.
  • Ask for a copy of your information, or ask us to correct it, by emailing SUPPORT_EMAIL.
  • Pixamoo can't be used to make pictures without an account, because every prompt goes through the safety check.

Children

Pixamoo is rated for ages 13 and up. It is not meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them. If you think a child under 13 has an account, email us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above, and tell you in the app if the change is significant.
